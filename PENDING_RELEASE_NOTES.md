- Let `rimworld/spawn_thing` spawn pawns. A pawn race such as `Muffalo` or a pawn kind such as `Colonist` now gives a generated pawn with the kind's default faction. Before, a pawn race failed with an error.
- Spawn things with `rimworld/spawn_thing` as the game's own debug spawner does. A thing made from stuff, such as a wall or a bed, gets its default stuff, and a building belongs to the player. Before, RimWorld logged an error for the missing stuff, and colonists ignored the unowned building.
- Page the children in `rimworld/list_debug_action_children` and `rimworld/get_debug_action`. A generated submenu such as `Spawn thing...` has more than a thousand children, and one reply was too large for agent clients. Pass `offset` for the next page.
- Report traits and work priorities in `rimworld/get_pawn_details`. Traits come with degree and suppressed state. Work priorities list all work types, highest natural priority first, with priority, disabled and permanently disabled state, and whether the Work tab shows them. The reply also says whether manual priorities are on.
- Describe the x and z of `rimworld/get_cells_info`, `rimworld/frame_cell_rect` and `rimworld/screenshot_cell_rect` as the south-west cell of the rectangle. Before, the text said top-left. The rectangle extends north and east from that cell.
- List each label once in `rimworld/get_ui_layout`. Before, a label drawn from a TaggedString or with `Listing_Standard.Label` had two entries with the same rectangle.
- Report the correct screen rectangles for listing controls in `rimworld/get_ui_layout`, such as the checkboxes of the options dialog. Before, when a listing did not start at the left or top edge of its group, its controls were reported too far right or down.
- Add `rimworld/list_pawns`. It lists every spawned pawn on the current map with its role, current hostility, faction, position and job, so an agent can find prisoners, animals and raiders for `rimworld/get_pawn_details`. The reply is paged.
- Add `rimworld/list_buildings`. It lists the current map's buildings with position, the cells they cover, rotation, faction, hit points and power state, filtered by def, architect category or player ownership. An unknown def or category fails. The reply is paged.
- Add `rimworld/get_map_summary`. It reads the current map in one call: date, weather, temperature, active game conditions, wealth and pawn counts. The pawn counts use the roles of `rimworld/list_pawns`.
